    Controls

    Flying

    Drag look around · Caps Lock lock the mouse to look around · Click a creature or object for its info · Esc leave full screen
    E forward · both mouse buttons fast forward · D back · W / R left / right
    Space / C or X up / down
    Shift fast · Wheel zoom
    G fly through walls · J cinematic camera

    Walking

    ` drop to the ground from the camera and walk, or fly again
    E / D run / backpedal · W / R turn
    X strafe left · Space jump
    Drag with the left button to turn the camera; hold the right button to turn yourself too (then W / R strafe); both buttons run
    Wheel zoom · \ or numpad / walk / run
    In water: Space / C up / down; Space at the surface jumps out

    Going places

    / go to a zone, town, dungeon or raid
    Click the minimap to fly there
    O whole world · 1 / 2 / 3 continents
    T back to the start
    Click the coordinates to copy a link to the view

    The world

    Y / Shift+Y later / earlier · N local time
    L torch · V grass and flowers
    B colour grading · Z fog
    H name colours for Alliance or Horde
    I dungeon and raid names · M music and sound
    Tab world map, filled in where you've explored · Esc off a flight

    Screen

    Click creatures and objects for their details
    P screenshot, everything in view in full detail
    U hide the interface · K performance stats
